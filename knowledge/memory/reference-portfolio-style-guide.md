`~/os/knowledge/library/style_reference/Project_Writeup.pdf` is the voice calibration reference for portfolio content.

Target style: specific and evidence-driven, careful with uncertainty, no marketing language. PLAN.md in the portfolio repo points agents here for tone alignment.

**How to apply:** When writing or reviewing portfolio copy, project writeups, or the About
page, calibrate voice first. Prefer the committed prose distillations —
[[seluga-project-writeup]] and [[seluga-final-paper]] — over the PDF; they carry the same
voice, are readable without a PDF pass, and survive in the repo.
